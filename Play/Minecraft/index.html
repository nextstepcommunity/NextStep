Server Rules

Please read and follow these guidelines to maintain a great community

📋 General Compliance

You must follow your local laws, the Minecraft EULA, and the Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Since Minecraft and Discord are connected, NextStep's Discord rules also apply here.

⚡ Keep Performance Peaceful

No AFK farming, massive mob farms, lag machines, large hopper/redstone contraptions, or anything else that causes significant performance issues.

🌍 Global Bans

Bans are global across NextStep platforms. A ban on one platform may result in a ban on other NextStep-related platforms. You have the right to appeal at appeal

🚫 Cheating Not Allowed

No duplication glitches, cheating/hacking/exploiting, x-ray, bots, or cracked/hacked clients. Whitelisted clients: Vanilla, Fabric, Forge, Vivecraft, LambdaMod.

🤝 Keep Respectful and Fair

Unclaimed items are fair game, but no grave camping, TP killing, harassment near claims, blocking spawn, griefing, or any other disruptive behavior.
