Next Step Partnership Agreement

What is the goal of this partnership?

Mutual growth. We will provide a visible advertisement to your community, as we at Next Step confirm it is safe. In return, we expect the same from you. Besides that, we're open to deeper communication, which includes sharing info on moderation cases, warning about known threats, and sharing tips and tricks.

What is this partnership agreement and why do we need it?

We at Next Step are very careful about where we send our members. That is why we have made a partnership agreement. This agreement is nothing more than an acknowledgement that you understand that we expect your server to be a decent server, that we will check that occasionally, and that we will stop the partnership once the terms of this full agreement have been surpassed.

What does Next Step provide?

  • An easy-to-find, visible advertisement to your community, together with our confirmation that it is safe.
  • A direct channel to the staff team for all Next Step partnered communities.
  • Access to our case logs, which include every punishment ever made on Next Step, with evidence, all sorted nicely (which we expect to stay private. Information leaks will be treated as a violation of this agreement), for one member called a partnership representative. (The partnered community chooses their representative.)

What do we expect from your community?

  • An advertisement that is easy to find and visible (either by being a post in a forum or a dedicated channel for Next Step). The advertisement cannot be buried under text or other things.
  • Partner communities must include and enforce at minimum the following rules:
    • You must follow the laws that apply to you.
    • You must follow Discord's official Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
    • NSFW content is strictly forbidden.
      • Note: This includes everything within the community.
    • Respect others.
    • No role abuse.
    • Encouraging, glorifying, or normalizing harmful behavior is strictly forbidden.
    • No harassment, hate, or drama.
      • Venting and expressing frustration can be allowed, provided they do not target, harass, or encourage hatred toward specific people.
  • Decent logging. All moderation actions must be recorded in logs, at least containing (but not limited to):
    • The member involved and the reason for the action.
    • The action taken and its duration.
    • Relevant evidence or context.
    • The moderator responsible.
  • Access to the community for at least one of our community representatives to check the validation of this agreement.

We believe this to be the minimum requirements for an appropriate community. Partnered communities may also share their case logs or adopt additional rules from Next Step (which we only encourage) but are not required to do so.

What is a Community Representative?

A community representative is someone who can talk on behalf of their community. This will, for example, be regarding contact about the status of the partnership. Depending on the desired bond strength of the partnered communities, the community representative can also be notified of staff cases with mutual members or tips and tricks for communities. Next Step will assign at least one of its staff as a representative, and the partnered community can assign one of their own, who will receive the partnership representative role.

Changes, Exemptions, and Violations

Next Step may propose changes to this agreement when necessary to maintain appropriate safety standards. Material changes require both parties' agreement. If they cannot agree, either party can end the partnership.

An exemption must be explicitly approved by both communities. Exemptions apply only to the specific requirement approved.

Accidental or minor breaches will most likely result in a friendly reminder. But knowingly violating this agreement without notifying the Next Step Representative may result in the immediate termination of this partnership and possibly the removal of any members connected to the staff of the ex-partnered community.

Acceptance

By accepting this partnership, both parties confirm that they have read, understood, and agreed to this agreement. The partnership remains conditional on continued compliance.
