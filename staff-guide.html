Welcome to the Next Step Staff Team

Welcome to the Next Step Staff Team. This guide explains what it means to be staff at Next Step, what is expected of you, how staff should handle difficult situations, and how each division contributes to the community.

Being staff is not about having a special role, access to private channels, or moderation permissions. Being staff means being trusted with responsibility.

You represent Next Step whenever you act as a staff member. Your actions can affect how safe, welcoming, and trustworthy the community feels.

Being staff is about more than having a role or permissions. It is about giving your time and effort to support others and help make this community better.

If you are here only for the badge, status, or power, this probably is not the right role for you. We would rather have a smaller team of people who genuinely believe in the mission than a large team that does not.

If you believe in what Next Step is trying to build and want to help create something meaningful, we are proud to have you on the team.

Welcome aboard.

About Next Step

Next Step is a mental health and personal growth community founded by Triggerhappy.

The community was created after an unrelated addiction-support server fell apart because of power abuse and other problems within its community.

After that experience, Next Step was built from the ground up. No previous experience, no large audience, and no major advantages.

It started simply: one member at a time.

The goal is to create a safe and active place where people can talk about their struggles without feeling judged, while connecting with others who may understand what they are going through.

Next Step provides peer and community support. We are not therapists, doctors, counselors, or emergency services, and staff should never present themselves as such.

Our long-term vision

We want Next Step to grow into a recognizable community and eventually a brand that stands for:

  • Health
  • Growth
  • Support
  • Safety
  • Genuine human connection

Even as we grow, one thing will remain important: Next Step should remain free and accessible.

We are not building Next Step around paid membership or making people pay to receive support. We want people to be able to access this community regardless of their financial situation.

Core Staff Principles

These principles apply to every staff member, regardless of role, division, or permissions.

🛡️ Safety

Member safety and community safety come first. If something creates an immediate risk to a member or the community, take appropriate action and notify higher staff.

⚖️ Fairness

Apply rules consistently. Do not punish someone more harshly because you dislike them, disagree with them, or have had previous problems with them.

🤝 Respect

Remain respectful even when members are difficult. Rules can be enforced firmly without insulting, mocking, humiliating, or threatening people.

📝 Accountability

Staff are not above the rules. If you make a mistake, acknowledge it and report it when necessary. Hiding mistakes is worse than making them.

🔎 Transparency

Important moderation actions should be documented and logged. Staff decisions should be understandable and reviewable by other staff members.

🔒 Confidentiality

Information obtained through staff work must remain private. Only share confidential information when it is necessary for legitimate Next Step staff work.

🚧 Boundaries

You are a community staff member, not a therapist, doctor, police officer, or crisis counselor. Support people without taking responsibility for their entire wellbeing.

🧠 Good Judgment

Not every situation can be solved by blindly following a rule. Think before acting, consider the circumstances, and ask another staff member for help when you are unsure.

Global Staff Responsibilities

These responsibilities apply to every member of the staff team, regardless of division.

Every staff member must:

  • Know and understand the 📜 Rules, ❔ Staff Guide, and Discord Community Guidelines.
  • Keep confidential staff and member information private.
  • Remain neutral and fair, especially during heated situations.
  • Act on serious rule violations instead of ignoring them.
  • Communicate with other staff when an issue requires cooperation or escalation.
  • Stay reasonably active both as a staff member and within your assigned division.
  • Complete tasks assigned to your division.
  • Log moderation actions in the appropriate division channel and in 🗄️ actions-log.
  • Properly document important incidents so other staff can understand what happened.
  • Inform staff if you will be inactive for an extended period.
  • Admit mistakes and take responsibility for them.
  • Ask for help when you are unsure instead of making a risky decision alone.
  • Escalate serious or unusual situations to the appropriate higher staff member.
  • Avoid using staff permissions for personal disputes or personal gain.
  • Respect member privacy and only access information necessary for your responsibilities.

Staff members should:

  • Suggest improvements, ideas, and useful tasks.
  • Set a good example through their own behavior.
  • Help members whenever possible or redirect them to a colleague in 🤝 Community.
  • Welcome new members and help them get started.
  • Test new features when asked.
  • Run /bump in ⏫ bump whenever possible.
  • Help other divisions when their workload allows it.
  • Share useful feedback with the staff team.
  • Look for ways to improve the community rather than only reacting when something goes wrong.

Working across divisions

Divisions exist to organize responsibilities, not to create isolated teams.

All staff members are encouraged to communicate and help with other divisions whenever possible.

For example, you do not have to be part of the Community Division to welcome a new member or help someone find the right channel.

Good staff work is teamwork.

Moderation Standards

Moderation is one of the most important responsibilities on the staff team. Use your permissions carefully and always consider whether your action is necessary and proportionate.

🚫 Things staff should never do

  • Never perform serious punishments such as kicking or banning unless you are an Admin or have approval from at least 1 Admin or 2 Moderators.
  • In urgent situations, moderators may take immediate action when necessary to protect the community, but the action must be reported afterward.
  • Never threaten members. Warnings and legitimate moderation consequences are allowed; intimidation is not.
  • Never insult, mock, humiliate, or deliberately provoke users.
  • Never argue with members simply to win an argument.
  • Never punish someone because of personal disagreements, friendships, grudges, or outside conflicts.
  • Never leak confidential staff or member information.
  • Never ignore serious rule violations because the person involved is a friend or someone you like.
  • Never speak officially on behalf of Next Step without permission to do so.
  • Never abuse, misuse, or test permissions on real members without a legitimate reason.
  • Never use staff access to investigate someone's private information out of curiosity.

When you are unsure

If you are unsure whether something deserves action, do not guess when the consequences could be serious.

Pause, gather the relevant information, and ask another staff member for advice.

Asking for help is good staff behavior. Making a preventable serious mistake because you were afraid to ask is not.

Handling Difficult Situations

Staff will eventually encounter situations that are confusing, emotional, or stressful. You are not expected to solve everything alone.

🧩 If you are unsure what to do

Stop and assess the situation before acting. Ask another staff member when necessary and escalate serious situations to the appropriate higher staff.

🔥 If a conflict is escalating

Keep your own emotions under control. Do not turn the situation into a personal argument. Separate the people involved when appropriate and focus on resolving the actual issue.

💬 If a member needs support

Listen, be respectful, and take them seriously. You do not need to have all the answers.

If the situation is beyond what community staff can reasonably handle, involve the appropriate staff member and encourage the person to seek professional or emergency support where appropriate.

🛡️ If there is an immediate safety concern

Prioritize safety over normal procedures. Take reasonable action to protect the community, notify appropriate higher staff, and document what happened afterward.

🧠 If you are personally affected

You are allowed to step back.

Staff members are people too. If a situation is personally affecting you, hand it over to another staff member rather than forcing yourself to handle it.

Staff Divisions

Our staff team is divided into specialized divisions. Choose divisions that match your skills, interests, and availability.

📋 Management

Keeps the staff team organized. Maintains staff information, coordinates responsibilities, helps staff work together, and makes sure important information stays up to date.

🛡️ Security

Keeps the community safe. Handles rule enforcement, reports, conflicts, harmful content, investigations, and other community safety concerns.

💻 IT

Takes care of the technical side of Next Step, including websites, bots, servers, automation, technical systems, troubleshooting, and development.

🎨 Design

Improves how Next Step looks and feels. Creates graphics, layouts, visual assets, branding, and helps maintain a consistent visual identity.

📢 Marketing

Helps more people discover Next Step. Works on promotion, outreach, partnerships, social media, and other ways to responsibly grow the community.

🤝 Community

Focuses on members and the community atmosphere. Welcomes people, supports healthy interactions, keeps the community active, and helps members feel included.

💼 Legal

Helps Next Step make responsible decisions by researching relevant laws, platform rules, policies, requirements, and potential legal or organizational risks.

💵 Finance

Manages money and resources. Tracks costs, payments, budgets, and helps the community make responsible financial decisions.

🎮 Next Step Play

Builds and manages gaming experiences connected to Next Step. Maintains game servers, creates activities, and improves the player experience.

🎉 Events

Plans and coordinates community events and activities. Works with other divisions to create engaging experiences and keep the community active.

Multiple divisions

You can be part of multiple divisions if your skills, interests, and availability allow it.

Being in multiple divisions does not mean you have to do everything. Quality and reliability are more important than collecting roles.

The How Can I Help Bot

Being staff comes with a lot of tasks and responsibilities. With multiple divisions and different projects, it can be difficult to know what you should work on.

That is why we created the How Can I Help Bot.

🤖 How it works

Simply run:

/todo

Then select your division. The bot will help show you tasks and ways you can contribute.

Not sure how you can help?

Start with the bot, check your division's responsibilities, or ask another staff member.

You do not need to wait for someone to tell you exactly what to do every time. If you notice something that could be improved, bring it up to the team or suggest a task.

Staff is about responsibility.

You are trusted with tools, information, and influence.

Use all three to help make Next Step a better place.

One Last Thing

You do not need to be perfect to be a good staff member.

You will make mistakes. You will encounter situations you have never dealt with before. You will sometimes get something wrong.

What matters is how you respond.

Ask for help. Learn from mistakes. Treat people fairly. Communicate with your team. Take responsibility.

Most importantly, remember why the staff team exists in the first place: to help build a community that people can trust.

Thank you for being part of Next Step.
