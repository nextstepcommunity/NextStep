Partnership Agreement

A shared commitment to safe, respectful, and supportive community growth.

1. Purpose and Mutual Growth

The purpose of this partnership is for both communities to grow in membership, knowledge, and experience through advertising, cooperation, and shared learning.

A partnership is conditional on both parties meeting this agreement. Either party may suspend or terminate the partnership but must notify the other party when doing so.

2. Advertising and Visibility

Both parties must provide visible advertising for the other community.

  • Advertisements must be easy to find.
  • Advertisements must be placed in the format of a form (The Next Step representative will be able to assist on this matter if necessary) or a designated channel for only the Next Step advertisement.
  • Advertisements cannot be buried under large amounts of text.
  • Alternative advertising arrangements require mutual agreement.

3. Community Representative

The partner community must designate at least one Next Step staff member as its representative.

The representative must be able to view moderation case logs to assess whether the community is enforcing its rules fairly, but is not required to participate in conversations or actively monitor every channel. Their access exists to verify compliance with this agreement.

The partner community must not deliberately conceal channels or relevant activity. The representative must be able to see all relevant channels, while respecting confidentiality and following the partner community's applicable rules.

4. Minimum Community Standards

Partner communities must include and enforce at least the following rules:

  • You must follow the laws that apply to you.
  • You must follow Discord's official Community Guidelines (https://discord.com/guidelines) and Terms of Service (https://discord.com/terms).
  • NSFW content is strictly forbidden.
  • Respect others.
  • No role abuse.
  • Encouraging, glorifying, or normalizing harmful behavior is strictly forbidden.
  • No harassment, hate, or drama.

Note: This includes your profile, messages, attachments, status, and any other content or activity within the community.

Venting and expressing frustration are allowed, provided they do not target, harass, or encourage hatred toward specific people.

These are minimum requirements. Partner communities may maintain their own rules or adopt additional rules from Next Step (which we only encourage), provided all the rules above are included.

5. Moderation, Records, and Next Step Members

Partner communities must enforce their rules consistently and fairly. Rules apply equally to all members, including staff, owners, and Next Step members.

Staff must not abuse their permissions to unfairly target, protect, or punish anyone. Moderation decisions must consider available evidence, applicable rules, and the circumstances. Serious or urgent safety concerns must be addressed appropriately.

All moderation actions must be recorded in accessible logs containing, at minimum:

  • The member involved and the reason for the action.
  • The action taken and its duration.
  • Relevant evidence or context.
  • The moderator responsible.

Records must be accurate and must not be deliberately altered or withheld to mislead Next Step during an audit.

When a Next Step member is involved in a moderation case, we request that the representative be notified so they can observe the process and raise concerns about unfair or inconsistent treatment. This does not grant Next Step members immunity from the partner community's rules.

The partner community retains authority over its moderation decisions. Urgent actions must never be delayed while waiting for the representative; notification should follow as soon as reasonably possible.

6. Partnership Audits

The Next Step representative may periodically audit the partner community to verify compliance. Audits may include:

  • Checking advertising arrangements.
  • Verifying required community rules.
  • Reviewing channels for rule violations and moderation.
  • Reviewing selected moderation cases and their records.
  • Paying particular attention to cases involving Next Step members.

Audits must be conducted responsibly and only for partnership oversight. Next Step may request clarification or supporting information when concerns arise.

7. Changes, Exemptions, and Violations

Next Step may propose changes to this agreement when necessary to maintain appropriate safety standards. Material changes require both parties' agreement. If they cannot agree, either party can end the partnership. Immediate temporary safety measures can still be taken when necessary, with the other party notified promptly.

Any exemption must be explicitly approved by Next Step staff. Exemptions apply only to the specific requirement approved.

Knowingly violating this agreement without notifying Next Step may result in severe consequences.

Accidental or minor breaches do not automatically warrant punishment. If the partner community discovers an unintentional breach, it must notify the other community as soon as reasonably possible after discovering the breach.

Consequences depend on the circumstances and may include a request for corrective action, suspension, or termination of the partnership.

8. Acceptance

By accepting this partnership, both parties confirm that they have read, understood, and agreed to this agreement. The partnership remains conditional on continued compliance.
